FAQ

Questions.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Yes — Apple Watch is what hears your day. Without one you can still open Twicy and try the full flow with sample data (Settings → Load sample data), but your own sentences come from the watch.

Which languages does it support?

You learn English. Your own language can be Japanese, Korean, or Chinese (Taiwan). The app itself is in English.

Why do I need an AI key? What does it cost?

Twicy has no servers, so it doesn't resell AI. You connect your own key from OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible provider. Twicy only sends short sentence text, so usage is light — check your provider's pricing for exact costs. You pay them directly; we never see the key.

Is it recording other people?

Your watch keeps only the parts where someone is speaking. With Voice ID on (the default), Twicy checks each sentence against your voice and drops what other people said. Recording is shown on the watch and you can stop it any time.

Does it work offline?

Listening, transcription, and practice run on your devices. Turning your day into cards needs a connection to your AI provider. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads its speech model.

Is it a subscription?

No. One purchase, and it's yours.
